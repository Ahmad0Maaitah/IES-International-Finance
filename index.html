About these materials

What they arePrepared by Dr Ahmad Maaitah in October 2026 for the IES Abroad London lecturer interview (FI 355 International Finance, 18 January to 23 April 2027). They are a proposal and a demonstration, not an official IES Abroad publication. Course facts (3 credits, 45 contact hours, no prerequisites, assessment weights) follow the IES Abroad course specification.
How to use the decksArrow keys or the toolbar move between slides; O shows an overview; F is full screen; S opens the speaker notes with timings. The sun and moon button switches to a light background for a bright room. Every number on a slide is sourced on the slide, and the two Big Mac prices in the demo can be typed in live when a new edition of the index appears.
Live examples of the same approachInteractive course sites I have built and run: MANG2074 Financial Econometrics and MANG3095 Research Project in Finance and FinTech (University of Southampton), the Bloomberg Challenge Hub (career and portfolio simulations), and the FinTech Professional Certificate (College of Banking and Financial Studies, Muscat).
